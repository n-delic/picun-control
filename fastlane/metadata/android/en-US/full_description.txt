Picun Control is an unofficial, offline, native replacement for the Picun Audio app.

It talks directly to Picun earbuds over Bluetooth LE and works without an account, without
internet access and without location tracking. The supported-model catalogue is bundled inside
the app, so nothing is downloaded at runtime.

Features:
- Low latency / game mode
- Noise cancelling, transparency and adaptive ANC
- 10-band equalizer with locally saved presets
- Battery, volume and playback controls
- Spatial audio, prompt language, find-my-earbuds
- Model-specific extras such as RGB light, heating, head tracking, touch controls, wear
  detection, wind-noise reduction, LDAC and sleep mode
- Diagnostics screen with a CRC-checked raw hex log for mapping new models

Only the game-mode toggle has been verified on real hardware so far. Other controls are
implemented from the reverse-engineered protocol and may behave differently on some models.
Unknown models stay read-only until the user explicitly enables experimental controls.

Picun Control is not affiliated with Picun.
